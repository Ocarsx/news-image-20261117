Sum up 17 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $250: $50 for 1st place, $30 for 2nd, $15 for 3rd, $5 each for 4th to 10th and $3 each for 11th to 50th.

The image is a newspaper front page for Tuesday 17 November 2026: the Kerch Bridge strike and the attack on Kyiv's Dnipro bridges; Iran's drone attack on the US radar base in Bahrain and mines near the Strait of Hormuz; the first Xi–Trump–Putin meeting confirmed for Shenzhen; Britain's sanctions on eight firms building settlements in the occupied West Bank; the JPMorgan software bug that halted online banking and Zelle; and the ULA Vulcan rocket failure that destroyed 40 Amazon Leo satellites. All text is drawn with PIL so every letter is exact; the six illustrations are AI-generated scenes with no text in them.
